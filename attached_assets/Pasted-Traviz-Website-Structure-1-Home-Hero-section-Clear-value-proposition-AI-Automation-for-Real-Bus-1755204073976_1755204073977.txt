Traviz Website Structure
1. Home

Hero section: Clear value proposition (“AI Automation for Real Business Impact”) + call-to-action.

Quick intro: Who you are & what you do in 2–3 sentences.

Featured services: Short teaser boxes linking to service pages.

Featured product(s): One-liner + “Learn more” link.

Key stats / impact: e.g., “1000+ hours saved for clients annually.”

Client logos or testimonials snippet.

Primary CTA: “Book a Consultation” / “Let’s Talk.”

2. About Us

Our Story: Short narrative of why Traviz exists.

Our Mission & Philosophy.

Core Values (with brief explanations).

Meet the Founder: Bio, photo, key credentials.

Our Approach: How you work with clients (e.g., Discovery → Design → Delivery → Support).

3. Services

Break down by core offerings, each with its own subpage:

AI Strategy & Consultation

Automation & AI Integration

Machine Learning & Engineering

Data Engineering & Science

AI Product Development

Each service page should include:

What it is (simple terms)

What you deliver

Example use cases

Client success snippet

4. Products

Intro section: “In addition to consultancy, Traviz develops AI-powered products…”

Product Spotlight:

Agentiq AI CRM: Short pitch, key benefits, link to dedicated site.

Future products (optional “Coming Soon” teaser).

5. Case Studies / Success Stories

One page with grid/list of stories (like your “Dental Receptionist AI Agent” example).

Each case study should include:

Client challenge

Our solution

The result (measurable impact)

Testimonial quote if available.

6. Testimonials

Pull from different industries to show versatility.

Include client names & companies (with permission).

7. Insights / Blog

Educational articles on AI trends, automation strategies, lessons from projects.

Can double as SEO growth channel.

8. Contact

Short message inviting contact.

Form: Name, Email, Message.

Optional: booking link for consultations.

Social media links.

9. (Optional) Resources

Free guides, checklists, whitepapers.

Builds credibility & lead capture.

Navigation Tip:
The top menu could be:
Home | Services | Products | Case Studies | Insights | About | Contact
